Who We Are

A Cork agency, built on the water's edge

Quigg Property was founded on a simple belief: that buying, selling or letting a home should be guided by honesty, deep local knowledge and a genuine commitment to results. Based in Cork, our roots run along the harbour — from Cobh to Crosshaven, Midleton to Kinsale — where we've helped hundreds of families and investors find their place.

Through our use of JamesEdition, we market bespoke properties to buyers in over 100 countries, combining that hyper-local expertise with genuine international reach — giving our clients the very best of both worlds.

Meet James Quigg, MIPAV

A career built on leadership, discipline, and execution.

James spent his career in the Defence Forces, holding key leadership roles where taking ownership of outcomes was essential. That experience shaped his approach to real estate: plan meticulously, communicate clearly, and follow through on every commitment.

With a relentless work ethic and meticulous attention to detail, James ensures nothing is left to chance—from the initial valuation to the day the keys hand over. Self-motivated and results-driven, he operates to a standard where you never have to chase for an update.
