Who We Are

A Cork agency, built on the water's edge

Quigg Property was founded on a simple belief: that buying, selling or letting a home should be guided by honesty, deep local knowledge and a genuine commitment to results. Based in Cork, our roots run along the harbour — from Cobh to Crosshaven, Midleton to Kinsale — where we've helped hundreds of families and investors find their place.

As part of the Keller Williams Global Reach network, we combine that hyper-local expertise with the tools, marketing reach and international referral network of the world's largest real estate franchise — giving our clients the very best of both worlds.

Meet James Quigg, MIPAV

A career built on leadership and discipline

James spent his career in the Defence Forces, where he held a number of leadership positions. Leading people and taking responsibility for the outcome taught him to plan carefully, communicate clearly and follow through on every commitment.

That background shows in the way he works. An excellent work ethic and close attention to detail mean nothing is left to chance, from the first valuation through to the day keys change hands. James is a self-motivated performer who sets his own standard and holds himself to it, so you never have to chase for an update.

Today, as Auctioneer & Property Advisor, he brings the same discipline to buying, selling and letting across Cork Harbour, with straightforward advice and steady communication at every stage.
