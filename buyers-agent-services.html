Buyers Agent Services

Someone In Your Corner

Representation that works for you, not the seller

In a typical sale, the selling agent represents the seller's interests. A buyer's agent works exclusively for you — searching the market, including properties that never reach the main portals, assessing real value, and negotiating firmly on your behalf.

This is especially valuable if you're buying from outside the area, short on time, or simply want an experienced, objective eye on a major financial decision. We bring local knowledge of Cork Harbour and the wider county, with international reach through JamesEdition, which markets bespoke properties in over 100 countries.

It starts with a free, no-obligation discovery call, and runs as one seamless service all the way through to legal completion — search, viewings, negotiation, survey and legal referrals all coordinated for you, so nothing falls between the cracks.

A Seamless Service

Every step, handled for you

From your first call to the day you collect the keys, here's exactly what's included.

1. Free Discovery Call

A one-hour, no-obligation call where we get to know what you're looking for and outline every step of the process ahead, so you know exactly what to expect.

2. Search & Seek

Based on your brief, we actively search the areas and property types you're interested in — including opportunities that never reach the main portals.

3. Viewings

We arrange and attend viewings on shortlisted properties, giving you honest, experienced feedback on each one.

4. Negotiation

Once you've found the right property, we negotiate directly on your behalf — working to secure the best possible price and terms.

5. Legal & Survey

We connect you with trusted solicitors and surveyors, who inspect the property and provide a detailed report before you commit.

6. Through to Completion

We stay involved end-to-end, coordinating every party so the process runs smoothly, right through to the keys in your hand.
