Buyers Agent Services

Someone In Your Corner

Representation that works for you, not the seller

In a typical sale, the selling agent represents the seller's interests. A buyer's agent works exclusively for you — searching the market, including properties that never reach the main portals, assessing real value, and negotiating firmly on your behalf.

This is especially valuable if you're buying from outside the area, short on time, or simply want an experienced, objective eye on a major financial decision. We bring local knowledge of Cork Harbour and the wider county, backed by Keller Williams Global Reach's network.

From first search through to key handover, we manage the process so you can make a confident, well-informed decision.
