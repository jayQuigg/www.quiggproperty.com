Probate & Life-Event Valuations

Independent & Professional

A clear, defensible valuation when it matters most

Not every valuation is about selling. Life brings moments that call for a formal, independent property valuation carried out properly — for a solicitor, a financial institution, the HSE, or your own peace of mind.

As a qualified Auctioneer & Property Advisor (MIPAV) with deep local knowledge of Cobh and the wider Cork market, James provides accurate, professional valuations referenced against real comparable sales, with a report you and your advisors can rely on.

Valuation Services

Where this kind of valuation is needed

Probate

An independent market valuation of a property as part of an estate, for solicitors and executors handling probate.

Fair Deal

A valuation for the Nursing Homes Support Scheme (Fair Deal), carried out to the standard the HSE requires.

Mortgage Restructuring

A current market valuation to support a mortgage restructure or re-mortgage with your lender.

Separation & Divorce

A fair, independent valuation to support separation or divorce proceedings, for you and your solicitor.

Every valuation is based on recent, comparable sales in your area through the official Property Price Register, combined with James's own local knowledge of condition, location and demand across Cobh, Cork City and County.
